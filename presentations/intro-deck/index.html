Talent market intelligence

Why strong candidates still get rejected

The technical isolation gap

Highly capable engineers without a business-analysis or stakeholder mindset. Verdict from hiring managers: “too technical”.

The strategic vs. tactical gap

Product owners and leads who live in high-level strategy. Verdict in agile, smaller teams: “not hands-on enough”.

The contract disconnect

Candidates want permanent (FTE); clients want contractor (OSVČ) frameworks. The strongest profiles withdraw over this, not over money.

We screen for these three before the first CV reaches you. Technical proficiency is table stakes; concise communication and working English decide the offer.

Observed across 144 applicants and 31 roles, 2025 · futureproof
