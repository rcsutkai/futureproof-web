The correct-invoice checklist

  • Your identification matches your agreement exactly — legal name, registered address, IČO/registration number, VAT ID where applicable.
  • Sequential, unique invoice number from your own numbering series.
  • Issue date = the date you actually submit.
  • Taxable supply date (DUZP) = the last calendar day of the worked month.
  • Approved timesheet attached where generated (Unit 1 always; Unit 2 where available).
  • Hours × rate per your agreement, currency as contracted.
Payment: due within the term stated in your agreement — typically 45 days from receipt of a correct invoice. In practice payment often lands faster, especially in your first months of cooperation. The clock starts when a correct invoice is received; an incorrect one is returned for fixing, so the checklist above is literally money.

VAT — pick your line

Czech VAT payer

Apply the standard Czech VAT rate of 21%.

Intra-EU VAT-registered

Apply 0% VAT and include the mandatory clause: "Subject to reverse charge mechanism". Your VAT ID (country prefix + number) must appear on the invoice.

Non-EU entity

Invoice per the applicable cross-border service tax treaty — align with your tax adviser; we'll confirm the layout works on our side.

Tooling tip: cloud invoicing tools with native Czech/EU tax alignment — Fakturoid, SuperFaktura or similar — get all of the above right by default. We're happy to check your first invoice layout before you send it.
