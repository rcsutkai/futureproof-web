The Scaut check, in detail

Scaut is an independent Czech verification provider with audited data storage. It confirms facts, not quality — nothing about your performance is assessed.

Scaut's own guide for candidates — what to expect, step by step (video)
One-shot entry. Once you submit the Scaut form there is no way back into the portal to fix a typo. A mis-clicked date picker or a wrong digit in a phone number is read by an automated system as an inconsistency and raises a flag. Read every date, phone number and email back before you submit.

Only five things are verified

For each employment: the employer, your contact there, that contact's function, your start date and your end date. That's the whole list. Salary, rate and performance are out of scope — you may black them out on any document you attach.

Do

  • Enter your name and document numbers exactly as on the document — photograph, don't type from memory.
  • Check that any evidence you attach (agreement, payslip, payment confirmation) clearly shows the dates you entered.
  • Give named contacts you know for employment verification, not generic hr@company addresses that often never reply.
  • Warn your contacts in advance that Scaut may email or call them to confirm your dates.
  • Cover your history without gaps; approximate months are fine.
  • Answer any Scaut clarification the same day — each round-trip costs a day or two.

Don't

  • Don't rush the date pickers — the most common flag is a calendar mis-click, not a wrong fact.
  • Don't abbreviate employer names; use the legal name you contracted with.
  • Don't leave optional fields empty when you have the information — it usually means a call later.
  • Don't worry about salary or rate on evidence — black it out; it isn't checked.
  • Don't be surprised by a phone call from Scaut — it's routine and usually means the check is nearly done.

Have these ready

Five minutes of preparation makes every step above faster.

Business identity

  • Registration number (IČO / national equivalent)
  • VAT ID with country prefix (or registration in progress)
  • Registered address exactly as in the register

For the background check

  • Identity document (passport or national ID)
  • Education & employment history consistent with your CV
  • Address history without gaps
  • Date of birth (needed to initiate the check)

Banking (can follow later)

  • Czech local account, or
  • IBAN + SWIFT/BIC, or
  • Account number + routing code + SWIFT (non-IBAN countries)
